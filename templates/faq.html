What can TransitGPT do?

TransitGPT is an AI assistant built on real Canadian transit data — covering 138 agencies across every province and territory. Here's what you can ask it.

Feature 1

Ask anything about travelling in Canada

Not sure how to get from one city to another? Just ask. TransitGPT combines data from the transit database with general knowledge to give you every option — local buses, trains, intercity coaches, rideshares, and more.

How do I get from Toronto to Ottawa?

From our transit database: GO Transit operates bus routes connecting Toronto-area stations toward Ottawa. Ontario Northland also has service on this corridor.

Other options to consider:
  • VIA Rail — trains run daily from Toronto Union Station to Ottawa (~4.5 hrs). Book at viarail.ca
  • FlixBus / Megabus — affordable intercity buses. Check flixbus.ca and ca.megabus.com
  • KangRide / PopaRide — Canadian carpooling apps, great for this route. Try kangride.com or poparide.com
  • By Car — about 4.5 hrs via Highway 7 or the 401
For official schedules and fares, visit gotransit.com or viarail.ca.

A note on conversation history
TransitGPT remembers the last 3 messages in your conversation so you can ask follow-up questions naturally — like "what about by train?" after asking about buses. After 3 exchanges the oldest message drops off, so for longer research sessions it helps to be specific in each question.
